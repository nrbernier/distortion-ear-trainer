## Idea

I want to build some kind of training tool to recognize different sorts of
sound distortion.

The idea is that you first chose a sound bite. Then you chose a certain kind of
distortion and can vary the level with a slider. There's a bypass switch to
listen to the original.

By default there should be different sound bites available with different
acoustic instruments (percussive, wind, violin, piano, voice, etc) as well as
some electronic sounds. It should also be possible to load user audio files.

The different kinds of distortion should be as exhaustive as possible. For
instance, frequency wobble, mp3 compression, bit quantization, sampling rate,
etc. For each one a small info bubble should be available with more info and a
link to wikipedia for full information. It should be possible to add multiple
kinds of distortion at once.
